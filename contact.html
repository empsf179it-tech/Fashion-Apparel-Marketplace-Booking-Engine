Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about booking fashion services and managing appointments.

How to book a fashion service? +

Simply navigate to the Booking page, select your desired service and provider, choose an available date and time, and confirm your request by paying the deposit.

How appointment availability works? +

Providers set their own schedules. Once you select a date on the calendar, the system will instantly display the remaining time slots available for that specific professional.

How quotes are calculated? +

Pricing dynamically updates based on the service's base rate and any selected optional add-ons, ensuring complete transparency before confirmation.

How deposits work? +

A 30% deposit is required to secure your appointment. This guarantees your slot. The remaining balance is handled directly with the provider upon completion.

How to change or cancel a booking? +

You can reschedule your appointment up to 48 hours prior without losing your deposit. Cancellations within 48 hours may forfeit the deposit amount.

How fashion professionals can join? +

Professionals can apply using the "Become a Fashion Partner" form. Our curation team will review your portfolio and reach out for onboarding.
